RankRadar — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 19, 2026

RankRadar ("we", "the extension") is an ASO keyword analytics tool for Apple App Store and Google Play. This policy describes what data the Chrome extension and its backend service collect and how it is used.

What we collect

  • Account data. Your email address and a salted hash of your password (bcrypt). We never store plain-text passwords.
  • The URL of the active store page. When you open the extension on an App Store or Google Play page and start an analysis, the URL of that page is sent to our backend to identify the app being analyzed. We do not read, track, or transmit your general browsing history.
  • Usage events. Anonymous product events (extension opened, analysis started/completed, payment started/succeeded, report downloaded) linked to your account, used to improve the product.
  • Payment status. Payments are processed by our payment provider; we store only the payment status and amount. We never see or store your card details.

What we do NOT collect

  • Browsing history or content of any pages other than the store page you explicitly analyze.
  • Cookies or data from other sites.
  • Any personal data of app users — all analytics concern public app-store listings only.

Permissions explained

  • activeTab / tabs — to read the URL of the store page you are viewing so the analysis can start with one click.
  • storage — to keep you signed in and cache your report settings locally in your browser.
  • Host access to our backend — the extension talks only to our own API service.

Data retention & deletion

Report data and account data are retained while your account is active. To delete your account and associated data, email us at the address below — we remove it within 30 days.

Third parties

We use a hosting provider to run the backend and a payment provider to process subscriptions. Aggregated, non-personal keyword metrics are computed from publicly available app-store data.
